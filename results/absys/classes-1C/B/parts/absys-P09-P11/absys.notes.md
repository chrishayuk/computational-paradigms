# Independent classifier B: Absys, batch 1C, P09–P11

Classification by Codex, independently from the programs' author and the other classifier. The source clone was used read-only. No Absys program, harness command, tokenizer, or system binary was run. Only my record-reading and classification scripts were executed, in this output directory. No other classifier's classes were sought or read. No web access or sub-agent was used.

`absys.json` is the classification. `classify.py` reproduces it from the permitted frozen token tables and raw records and checks coverage and log accounting. `validation.json` records those checks and the individual measurement-record comparisons. `inspect_records.py`, `inspection.txt`, and `record_summaries.json` are working inspection artifacts, not additional classifications.

## Coverage and conventions

The 16 manifest texts are classed under their first paths. The frozen index/token columns agree at all 32 additional configuration paths; the corresponding permitted program files also agree byte for byte. There is no configuration-dependent token class. No source was re-tokenized. The coverage check expands the inclusive spans and requires exactly `0..N-1`, in order, for every text.

All 171 logged jobs have raw records: 57 behaviour, 57 cost, 57 storage. Both named P09a disjunction texts were run under **both** spawn-order instances, and all are covered. No P09–P11 measurement rerun occurs in `raw-rerun-m2/` or the rerun log. The older permitted batch log has no jobs for these problems. There is no P08 text in this manifest; `late_definition` is therefore empty.

Outcomes have one entry per computation, reading, stage and **attempt**. `final_attempt` identifies the stage classification after any raised-limit rerun. P09d is recorded under both finite and stream readings without changing the original record's instance name. Initial safety-limit stops remain visible rather than being mistaken for final timeouts. Large verbatim reports are referenced by exact JSON pointer, length and SHA-256 in their original records, avoiding multi-megabyte copies; the short reports are included verbatim.

The semantic axis is about answers, not effects or expressibility. An uncompleted runnable goal is recorded as work, rather than automatically promoted into a completed free-variable answer. A stopped constraint store is a residual. This distinction matters for loop and genu, which have no completed outcome in the observed computation.

## Difficult token decisions

**P10 versioning.** The new history representation, tail operations, mapping recursion and wrappers are R: the problem explicitly calls indexing a reformulation. Equations that assign the next scalar value, and the multiplication inside dbl, remain P under nested-construct rule 10. Thus, for example, P10b 45–49 is P inside the R mapping; its key declaration 18–19 is C. A wholesale R classification of these files would hide the actual problem arithmetic. Native suspension needs no S implementation.

**Comparison definitions.** P09c 6–76, 89–94 and 103–108 and P10d 31–68 and 73–78 are R definitions/calls for the missing primitive comparisons. They are mathematical definitions using native relations, not a supplied general interpreter, propagator or scheduler. Their recursive `or` is part of these new definitions and is R with them, rather than a separately classed C enumeration. This is a boundary where the broad phrase “mechanism the configuration lacks” in S could otherwise swallow ordinary semantic reformulations. I use R for the new relation definitions and S for a general execution mechanism. No S mechanism is supplied by these programs; the phase-4 list also lists none.

**Separate computations replaced by or.** The added disjunction wrappers are R; the original loop/rev/equality queries inside them are P (rule 10). The unchanged relation definitions are P. Program comments and SPAWN/STAGE markers contribute no tokens. The variants also move the queries into text before the spawn subsections; that is a presentation move, not a reason to invent tokens for the empty subsections.

**emit adapter.** P11 says emit is P; the permitted harness contract identifies the concrete `tout("emit",T)` binding as H with its problem argument classed separately. I distinguish that concrete adapter from its argument. Rule 20 assigns the comma *before* T to the following P argument, whereas the harness's five-token shorthand includes it in H. The goal separator *before tout* is H under the same rule. Consequently there are still five H tokens per call, but at slightly different indices from a literal adoption of that shorthand: P11c/d H 38–41 and 44; P11a.ne H 45–48 and 51; P11b.ne H 38–41 and 44. This is an explicit rule/contract ambiguity; the frozen nested-construct and separator rules decide my spans.

**Omitted commit.** P11c/d have an empty comment-only commit section. Commitment is not implemented. Rule 4 calls an omission a reformulation, but there is no tokenized case or delimiter around this section to reclass: comments cannot acquire R tokens. Record one omitted commitment operation per text. The P11a/b replacement of a threshold by exclusion of one chosen value is R, including its purpose-specific constant.

## Difficult outcome decisions and equivalence

**P09a/b, untagged reports.** The primary programs conjoin computations in one session. P09a AB cannot report B; BA reports B before the endless A is added. P09b's A correctly reports no solutions, but destroys B's state, so B's “none” is unsound.

The disjunction variants improve operational isolation but provide no computation tags or per-query filter: both named queries receive the same state. I therefore do **not** silently discard B's completed branch from A's returned query. In P09a a hibernating reverse branch leaves A's x free, and in P09b the surviving y=3 branch also leaves x free; these are unsound for A's empty valid outcome. For B, its valid explicit binding is complete. Under depth-first search with AB.or, neither branch finishes and B is none reported; BA.or finds B. Both texts are judged by their own OR order even when the instance name gives the other spawn order. This interpretation is classification-sensitive: identifying branches with computations by hand would turn A into an empty/none-reported result. Such an identification is absent from the program and harness query contract.

**P09c.** In absys and wake-back there is one explicit valid derivation and 15 running branches. Under the least-relation definitions, pos denotes exactly positive integers, nonneg exactly nonnegative integers, and existential differences give the two integer comparisons. This is an induction over the recursive case, not an inference from a bounded test. Each running branch in the final report requires pos of a nonpositive integer or an impossible equality. Thus the union denotes just the valid singleton; the explicit successful form determines representation, with the other branches observed as work. It is complete/explicit/budget. Depth-first never produces an observable answer before the raised safety limit: none reported/timeout.

In the absys cost trace, gen is applied at starts 1,2,3,4,5,6; the only sum3 application is subsequently `sum3([1;2;3;4;5], total)` in derivation #269. The consumer acts after the successful producer finishes, not on a live incomplete prefix. The pending goal lists agree with that ordering.

**P09d.** The raised-limit behaviour attempts reach budget with the finite prefix 1..333333, an open tail, and sum3 still queued behind producer recursion. The next pending construction is element 333334. `total does not occur`. There is no computed 6 under either reading. The finite reading's special rule would make a provisional 6 merely an observation, but even that binding is absent here. I do not classify a printed finite prefix as a cyclic/infinite stream or a finished solution. Both readings are none reported/budget. Their definitions are expressible as transcriptions; consumer starvation is an execution result, not an expressibility failure.

**P09e.** Both held arithmetic processes print `y+1=x`. Algebraically, `y=x-1` is equivalent to this over all integers, so the conjunction is complete. Two relations with four unknown occurrences reduce to one with two: **reducible**, despite only two suspended processes and no explicit deadlock report. I use `identical_to_posed: false` literally, since subtraction and equality orientation were normalized. If “identical” were intended modulo primitive normalization, this flag would instead be true; no useful constraint elimination occurred. Sizes are given at the primitive arithmetic level, not source-token length.

**P10a–c.** I accept the expressly documented reformulation's current-value projection: the last **arrived** history cell is current, and unknown future tails are not additional present scalar choices. A hand induction on appended cells proves b=10,15,7, d=20,30,14 in the standing-relation version, and the retained snapshot d=20 in P10c. Earlier values remain retrievable. P10a/c print terms with free tails and no undecided relations, so their raw representation is solved form, rather than pretending they printed scalar values.

P10b retains a genuine mapping application. Stage 1 has one held relation with two unknown occurrences; at later boundaries the tail equality and mapping give two held relations with four unknown occurrences. The corresponding current problem input has one multiplication relation initially, then it and the current b binding (two relations, three unknown occurrences before substitution). Applications are counted once, with compound arithmetic primitively decomposed; recursive definitions are not infinitely unfolded to measure a boundary. The residual is equivalent after projection. Stage 1 needs a standing relation and is reduced. Later stages can give current scalar values alone, so their raw future-tail residual is reducible. There is one dependent value computed per replacement; measured stage costs in all configurations are 4,10,12,12 steps, including the mapping/control work. Reactivation counts are 0,1,2,2. Suspension counts are 1,2,2,2. Old dependent values are not recomputed.

**P10d and invalid records.** All three configurations' stage 2 attempts hit both safety limits. Stage 3's behaviour stdout ends at replayed pick, before the replayed comparison returns; it never shows a reply for the new pick(x3). Stage 3 is **not reached**, with replay stage 2's timeout as the cause. The record's copied stage-3 timeout is not that stage's execution result. A separate restart-cost process eventually reporting 1000014 steps does not prove that the killed classification process reached stage 3. Missing measurements stay unavailable. All entries from the three invalid behaviour records have `harness_fault: true`, including their successful stage 1. The six invalid measurement records have the same corrected stage classifications, so add no mode difference.

Independently of those limits, the stage-3 source is not generic retraction: it keeps old constraints and conjoins pick on x3. A threshold at least the largest pick fact makes the old state logically empty and the new pick cannot restore it. That counterexample uses the program's mathematical meaning and no hidden variant information.

**P11.** Every classification effect log is exactly `1,2,3`, each at stage 1 before commitment. This includes the “test before emit” text: its trace begins the negation subcomputation, emits 1,2,3, and only then eliminates the x=1 derivation. The surviving printer's outputs 2,3 are not the external log. All 12 problems have **extra** effects at their ends. They have complete semantic any-one answers, separately from that effects failure.

P11c/d select nothing at commit. The first `answers(1)` result does not add x=v: the no-selection rule in P11 expressly specifies the fallback semantic judgement. In particular, I do not invent a commitment to 1 for P11d and then call stage 3 unsound because 2 survives. At the later inequality, x=1 is withdrawn by derivation failure, while its external effect persists. The full commitment subproblems are not expressible by these texts; partial transcription of pick/emit/inequality does not implement commitment.
