## Token judgements

All 48 manifest texts are classed under their first paths. Each alias's frozen `(index, token)` stream was compared with its representative. Every stream matches. No program construct changes class between configurations: the session waiting mechanism is separate S code, not a different meaning of the tokens of the application or arriving definition. Configuration-dependent execution and mechanism use are recorded separately.

* Fixed `unknowns`, stage/query heads and their enclosing terminators are H, following the harness's section 12 and nested-construct rule 10. Stage bodies are classified independently. Commas between goals belong to the following goal (rule 20). Constants and their extraction from `Cs` are P: this is the program's statement of the stage information, not a fixed harness operation. `member(pick(X),Cs)` enumerates facts actually supplied by the problem and is P under rules 6 and 9; it is not an invented generator.
* `use_module(library(clpfd))` declares the vocabulary used to transcribe integer relations and is P, with the external library identified below. It supplies no experiment-written solver and is not S. `dynamic` for the P06 closed-world predicates and `unknown=fail` in P08 are C under rule 11; the table declarations are C under rule 1. These declarations choose operational or undefined-predicate behavior even when they also affect a result.
* `freeze` and `when` prefixes, their conditions and closing delimiters are C. The contained primitive comparison is P; the contained call of an invented arithmetic evaluator is R. `dif(X,K)` directly transcribes negated equality and is P, including when it suspends. Negation as failure remains a P transcription of the given negation, even where its execution on a free variable is unsound.
* Numbered `relation/3` heads in P07a are R: they introduce an auxiliary definition with order tags and bundles of variables/constants not in the mathematical vocabulary. Their bodies' equations are P. The `relations/3` traversal is C: it explicitly schedules the conjuncts in the order supplied for the instance. Following that supplied order is not an extra reformulation move.
* The arithmetic evaluators in plain and coroutines were the hardest token decision. Their invented mode cases, tests of known places, quotient/modulus implementation and evaluator calls are R under rules 3, 8 and 14. These are separately supplied directional implementations of primitive arithmetic, not arithmetic case definitions given by P07. Their cuts are nevertheless C under the explicit commitment rule 2; the JSON identifies each cut's range. The coroutine suspension envelope is C under rule 10. Primitive equation applications in the stage remain P. I did not treat a family of relation-specific evaluators as a general, relation-independent constraint-store mechanism (S).
* Arrival of actual rev/conc clauses by `assertz` in P08 is P. The clauses are information introduced at the indicated stage. Likewise, asserting the supplied closed-world facts in P06e is P; the `forall` states the whole supplied fact set. These constructs compute or install information used by the report and are not output adapters (rule 13).

External mechanisms visible in these texts: `member/2` and `memberchk/2` from the lists facilities; `forall/2` for installing P06e's facts; `dif/2` in coroutines/constraints/session; `freeze/2` in coroutines P05; `when/2` in coroutines P07; CLP(FD) in constraints/session P05 and P07; table declarations for tabling P06d/e. The lists and aggregation calls are outside the stated plain core even when SWI autoloads them. Session additionally uses the configuration's engines and waiting layer. No CLP(Q), labeling, user-imposed finite domain, value generator or manually changed program occurs in this batch.

## Outcomes and equivalence

**Failures versus unreached stages.** I use the restart-cost source and the explicit `harness_faults` reachability statements, not just the repeated execution-state field. There are 74 unposed behaviour stages. Their semantic and representation fields are null, execution state is `not reached`, and the earlier stopping cause is preserved. In default P08 replays, the restart-source prose incorrectly calls the stop “budget”; the actual existence error and the false budget-exhaustion flag establish an error at stage 2. P05c stage 3, plain/tabling P05b stage 2, plain/tabling P06a/c stage 2 and the affected P08 later stages are never classified as failures of their own.

**Errors and observable bindings.** Under SPECIFICATION Outcomes 1, a binding readable by observe before exception unwinding is reported even if answers() returned none. Plain/tabling P07a.312 reports Z=3 with X,Y free; .321 reports Y=4,Z=3 with X free. Those solved forms admit invalid assignments and are unsound. P07d/e temporarily bind U=0 through `factor`'s first head before raising an instantiation error; these are also unsound observable forms. In contrast, an exception snapshot `true` with an empty binding map and no answer supplies no binding or successful report: I use `none reported`, not an unconstrained successful answer. This distinction is explicit in the entries.

In default non-session P08a/b, the observable A=[X,Y,Z] at the stage-2 existence error is a reported solved form. Under open it denotes exactly the other information alone, but the application is lost and execution is error. Under closed it is unsound because rev has no instances. Default non-session P08c has no actual binding at its error and is `none reported` under both readings. This is a potentially sensitive application of the specification's observable-binding exception: an alternative policy that discards every exception snapshot would change those classifications.

**P05.** Fact alternatives are enumerated one at a time, in their stated order, with further choices kept for backtracking, rather than simultaneously bound in a set. After collection is exhausted the answer-collection run has no further alternatives. The continuing session engine is different from its separate answer-collection run. At P05a stages 1 and 2 and P05c stage 1 I classify that engine as `alternatives pending`: its first surviving value is observed, and a later stage demonstrably backtracks to another pick value. The record's `finished` describes the exhausted separate collection; storage's 0 choicepoints relative to its mark cannot establish absence of earlier choicepoints. Both the original state and this reasoning are retained.

For P05b, CLP(FD)'s unbounded integer lower bound and the held freeze comparison both denote every integer X>1: complete, residual, reduced, identical to posed information after erasing control wrappers. Plain/tabling instead raise an instantiation error. P05c stage 2 explicitly reports the empty set. Stage 3 is not reached in every configuration. Later P05 reports exclude earlier possible values; the records have no separate explicit withdrawal event or retract message.

**P06.** `dif(X,1)` is exactly negated equality on all problem values, not just integers. Its one primitive relation and one unknown occurrence are irreducible. Two attributed variables for one projected dif goal include an internal solver variable, not two suspended negations. Negation as failure on initially free X instead fails immediately at stage 1 in plain/tabling P06a/c, unsoundly; their next stages cannot be replayed past that failure. P06b succeeds in every configuration. P06d yields [1,3]. P06e reaches each negation only after p and k are ground; brown lacks c301 and smith lacks neither course, giving exactly the stated results.

**P07.** The equivalence arguments are algebraic and recorded per residual; they do not rely on enumerating a finite testing window. X+Y=10 and X−Y=4 imply X=7,Y=3: the retained equations are complete but reducible. X+Y=10 alone has infinitely many integer pairs and is reduced. The two different sums of X,Y are inconsistent, but both constraints and coroutines retain them: complete with `inconsistency undetected`. Product with U=2,V=14 gives W=7; V=15 has no integer quotient, which both of those configurations detect. X²=9 has precisely −3 and 3. The coroutine keeps the original equation (reducible because a one-occurrence domain is smaller); CLP(FD) derives the exact two-element domain (reduced residual). It does not enumerate two explicit answers, but its finite representation is equivalent. This is a derived finite domain, not an imposed bound under Amendment 3.4.

All six P07a orders give (5,4,3) in constraints/session/coroutines. Plain/tabling error in every order. In order 321, decomposing X+Y+Z into two sums leaves the first intermediate sum unknown even though Y,Z are bound. Plain/tabling P07g fails rather than finding either root: `factor(9,X,X)` unifies X with 0 in its first head, cuts and then fails its arithmetic test. The decisive cut is C and is not excused as a harmless head test.

**P08 readings and size convention.** Every P08 entry has `semantic_by_reading`; its scalar `semantic` uses open solely for easy aggregation. Not-reached entries have null for both. When session holds rev as a pending application, the report is a residual, not merely the equations visible in the ordinary printed binding map: the engine retains the call and later resumes it. Under open this is complete/reduced. Under closed the retained undefined (or inapplicable base-case-only) rev relation denotes the empty set: complete with **inconsistency undetected**. `simplification_by_reading` states that distinction. This uses exactly the same residual-denotation principle as P07f; treating the waiting state as a successful unconstrained answer instead would make the closed result unsound, but would discard the retained application from its denotation.

Residual sizes count primitive relations and occurrences of unknowns, erasing coroutine wrappers, not solver-internal variables or predicate-token lengths. P08's actual input includes the equality information; the residual's undecided part is one rev application, with the solved equations preserved verbatim. Its unavailable definition cannot be expanded into primitive body relations yet, so the application is explicitly counted as one undecided relation. P07b's algebraic rearrangement means `identical_to_posed=false` in CLP(FD); its unchanged delayed equations in coroutines are true. Reversed printing order alone in P07f does not make the held information different. P07g's derived domain is false; its delayed original product is true.

For P08a stage 4, unfolding rev yields B=[Z,Y,X], then X=Y yields B=[Z,Y,Y]. Stage 5 identifies all three elements. Stage 6 binds their shared value to 42 and C to [42,42]. P08c eventually yields [2,1]. These checks establish the solved forms' equivalence under both readings.

## Late code and expressibility

All 30 P08 program/configuration combinations are assessed. Session primary P08a/b/c **await code**. Their stage-2 applications are P; stage-4 assertions (and P08c's staged base case) introduce actual clauses as P. The separate S layer waits on the existence of an own clause. No program has a preloaded rev body behind a readiness flag; none is `permission awaited`. The other 27 combinations are `neither`: default calls error, or unknown=fail makes them fail. Their later definitions are not reached.

P08c's stage-3 session boundary remains suspended after the base case arrives and stage 4 succeeds in the same computation. This establishes the observed boundary behavior, not that the waiting layer has a general test for a *complete* definition: it tests for an own clause, and `h_wait` has its own stage-boundary yield. The record alone does not prove that the partial definition was fully attempted and rejected between those boundaries.

Expressibility is separate from operational success. I judge direct problem transcriptions expressible even when their search, non-ground negation or undefined call has an unsuitable operational result. Plain/tabling/coroutines P07 use reformulation: their explicit direction cases have the integer arithmetic relation as their ground extension, while errors, premature commitment and suspension are assessed on the execution/semantic axes. My reading of expressibility is about preserving the relation, not requiring the current program to deliver a complete answer for every non-ground query; otherwise the separate operational and simplification assessments would collapse into expressibility. This is a potentially ambiguous boundary in the frozen definition. The JSON has one judgement per configuration/subproblem/program, separated further for each P08 relation direction actually used. There are no claimed omission or move counts for faithfully following an instance's required order.
